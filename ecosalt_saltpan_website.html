01 / The challenge

When saltpans are abandoned, ecosystems are affected.

Saltpans are important working landscapes and coastal habitats. Without timely monitoring and sustainable management, both livelihoods and biodiversity can be put under pressure.

🌊

Changing water conditions

Unmanaged water levels and high salinity can create hypersaline conditions and affect the surrounding environment.

🏚️

Saltpan abandonment

Lack of accessible monitoring and management support can make it harder to maintain saltpan operations over time.

🦩

Habitat pressure

Changes to wetland conditions can affect feeding and resting areas used by birds and other wildlife.

📉

Limited field information

When readings are not collected regularly, it becomes difficult to spot changing conditions and respond early.

02 / Our solution

Monitor. Understand. Act sustainably.

EcoSalt presents a concept for a simple digital monitoring system that turns field observations into clear information for better decisions.

📡

Smart monitoring

Use water-quality sensors and regular field readings to track salinity and water levels.

📊

Easy dashboard

View readings, trends, and alerts in one place so changes can be noticed earlier.

🌱

Responsible water management

Support informed water-management decisions to reduce the risk of prolonged hypersaline conditions.

🦆

Bird-friendly zones

Plan and monitor shallow-water and low-disturbance areas that can support wetland birds.

🔔

Condition alerts

Highlight readings that need attention and encourage timely field inspection.

🤝

Community support

Make useful information easier to share with saltpan workers, local groups, and conservation teams.
